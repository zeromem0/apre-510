Start menu

ButtonWhat it does
▲ ▼Choose: AprecID (first, chosen on entry), RS232 Tester, Settings. Wraps around at the ends.
OK, RUNStart the chosen one
AUTOHeld: About, what AprecID recognises. Any button goes back to the menu.

With Startup = AprecID (Settings), APRE-510 starts straight in AprecID, without the menu. At the bottom of the menu: the version, the clock (240 MHz) and the image check (“ok” or “BAD”).

Top bar “watching” with Hands-off (Settings) on: the line is watched and recognised again by itself when it changes, with a beep at each result. The result also goes out over USB, to the apre page or the Android app.

Single character view: ▲ ▼ move the threshold by hand, AUTO held puts it back in the middle of the levels; in HOLD or on a capture ◄ ► step through the characters instead of the range. Bus health view: RUN starts the figures again; it keeps its own rate (▲ ▼ do not change it).

Entered from AprecID, the tester starts on A (speed worked out from the line), at the rate AprecID read the line at; bus health takes the speed from the line and follows it if it changes. At 2 MS/s the screen still shows the line, but the decoder no longer keeps up with it.
